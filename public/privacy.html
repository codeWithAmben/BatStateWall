Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026 · How BatStateWall collects, uses, and protects information

Google OAuth Scope Pledge: BatStateWall only requests basic identity scopes (openid, email, profile). We never read, access, or store your Gmail messages, Google Drive files, Contacts, or Google passwords.

1. Information We Collect

When you sign in with Google OAuth 2.0 or participate on BatStateWall, we store only what is needed to run the wall and Sudoku Arena:

  • Google Account Profile: Your Google subject identifier (google_id), display name, verified email address, and profile image URL (avatar_url). Your email address is only visible to you in your account dropdown and is never shown publicly on wall posts.
  • Community Wall Activity: Messages you post, comments you submit, likes you toggle, and moderation reports you file.
  • BatStateU Sudoku Arena Data: Active and completed puzzle boards, pencil notes, hint/mistake counters, solve duration, scores, daily streaks, and public ranking positions.

2. How We Use Information

  • Authenticate your signed-in session and associate your posts, comments, likes, and Sudoku scores with your profile.
  • Display your chosen name, profile avatar, and Top-10 Sudoku rank badge alongside your public posts and leaderboard rows.
  • Run automated English & Tagalog profanity filtering on submitted names, posts, and comments.
  • Enforce server-side Sudoku puzzle integrity, mistake limits, and Daily Challenge fairness.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share user data with advertisers or data brokers.

3. Cookies & Browser Storage

  • Session Cookie (batstatewall_session): Essential HMAC-SHA256 signed cookie (HttpOnly, SameSite=Lax, and Secure over HTTPS) used strictly to keep you signed in for up to 7 days.
  • OAuth State Cookie (batstatewall_oauth_state): Short-lived (10-minute) CSRF protection cookie created during Google sign-in and cleared upon callback completion.
  • Local Storage (batstate_sudoku_muted): Optional client-side toggle remembering whether Sudoku sound effects are muted on your device. No third-party tracking or advertising cookies are used.

4. Infrastructure & Security

BatStateWall runs on Render (Web Service) backed by Supabase managed PostgreSQL over encrypted TLS connections with parameterized SQL queries and HMAC-signed session cookies. While no online platform can guarantee absolute security, we limit data collection to the minimum required fields and omit sensitive tokens and Sudoku solutions from public responses.

5. Your Rights & Data Control

  • Delete Your Posts: Open the overflow menu (… → Delete post) on any post you created to permanently remove it.
  • End Your Session: Click Log Out in your profile menu at any time to immediately clear your session cookie.
  • Revoke Google Access: Manage or revoke BatStateWall’s OAuth access anytime at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Account Deletion Requests: Contact the IT 412 project maintainers via your BatStateU course repository if you would like your account record permanently removed from the database.
