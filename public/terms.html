2. Eligibility & Google Account Access

  • Public browsing of the feedback wall and Sudoku leaderboards does not require signing in.
  • Creating posts, liking, commenting, reporting posts, and playing ranked Sudoku require authenticating via Google OAuth 2.0.
  • You are responsible for maintaining the security of your Google account and all activity performed under your active session.

3. Community Guidelines & Content Moderation

To keep BatStateWall welcoming, constructive, and safe for the Red Spartan community, all submissions must comply with these rules:

  • Keep messages respectful, constructive, and concise (within the 280-character limit).
  • Automated English and Tagalog profanity filtering is enforced across display names, posts, and comments. Attempting to bypass or evade moderation filters is prohibited.
  • Do not post hate speech, harassment, defamatory statements, doxxing/private personal data, spam, or impersonate faculty, staff, or other students.
  • Community members can flag questionable content via the post menu (… → Report post). Reported or abusive content may be removed without prior notice.

4. BatStateU Sudoku Arena & Fair Play

  • All Sudoku boards, move validation, hints, solve durations, and ranking calculations are enforced server-side.
  • You agree not to use automated bots, scripts, replay tampering, or modified API requests to manipulate timers, scores, Daily Spartan Challenges, or leaderboard standings.
  • Accounts found abusing gameplay endpoints or attempting unauthorized access to server data may be restricted or removed from public rankings.

5. User Content & Deletion

You remain solely responsible for the text you publish on BatStateWall. By posting publicly on the wall, you grant BatStateWall permission to display your post, display name, profile avatar, and Sudoku rank badge to visitors on the platform. You may remove your own posts at any time using … → Delete post.
